# Contributing

1. Open an issue describing the change (new notification, fix, sector overlay, example).
2. Edit `skills/dpdp-claude-dev-skill/SKILL.md`. Cite the section, rule, or notification for every legal claim.
3. Keep "the law requires" separate from "good practice".
4. Add a line to `CHANGELOG.md`.
5. Repackage `dist/` if you can; otherwise a maintainer will.
